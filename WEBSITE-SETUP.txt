AEROSTRIKE - PUT THE GAME ON A FREE PUBLIC WEBSITE
=====================================================

You need 3 free accounts. Total time: about 20 minutes.
  - GitHub  (stores your game files)          https://github.com
  - Upstash (keeps player accounts forever)    https://upstash.com
  - Render  (runs the game server/website)     https://render.com

How it behaves on the free plan:
  - The site goes to sleep after 15 minutes with nobody playing.
  - The next visitor waits about a minute while it wakes up, then it plays normally.
  - Player accounts and bot memory are kept in Upstash, so nothing is lost when it sleeps.


STEP 1 - Put the game files on GitHub
-------------------------------------
1. Sign up at github.com and click the "+" (top right) > "New repository".
2. Name it  aerostrike , choose Public, and click "Create repository".
3. On the new page click "uploading an existing file".
4. Open your  Documents\PortalArena  folder and drag EVERYTHING in it onto the page
   (server.js, package.json, render.yaml, .gitignore, start.bat, README.txt and the
   whole "public" folder). Do NOT upload accounts.json or roster.json.
   Tip: .gitignore is a hidden file. If you can't see it, it's fine to skip it.
5. Click "Commit changes".


STEP 2 - Make the free account database (Upstash)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Sign up at upstash.com (you can sign in with your GitHub account).
2. Click "Create Database" (Redis). Give it any name, pick the region closest
   to you, choose the FREE plan, and create it.
3. On the database page find the "REST API" section. Copy these two values:
     UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL     (starts with https://)
     UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN   (a long string)
   Keep the token secret - anyone with it can change your players' data.


STEP 3 - Start the website (Render)
-----------------------------------
1. Sign up at render.com with your GitHub account.
2. Click "New +" > "Blueprint", pick your  aerostrike  repository, and click "Connect".
   (Render reads the render.yaml file and sets everything up on the Free plan.)
3. It will ask for the two Upstash values from Step 2 - paste them in.
4. Click "Apply" / "Deploy". Wait for the log to say
     "AEROSTRIKE server is running!"  and  "Saving accounts to: Upstash cloud database"
5. Your game is live at the address Render shows, like
     https://aerostrike-xxxx.onrender.com
   Send that link to anyone - they can play from any computer.


UPDATING THE GAME LATER
-----------------------
When Claude gives you a new version, open your repository on GitHub, click
"Add file" > "Upload files", drag the changed files in (usually public/index.html
and sometimes server.js) and click "Commit changes". Render rebuilds the site
by itself in a minute or two. Player accounts are not affected.


HOW PLAYING WORKS ON THE WEBSITE
--------------------------------
- PLAY puts you in a queue for the mode on your Mode card. You join a public
  match with other real players, and bots fill any empty spots.
- Custom Game > "Create private room" gives you a 4-letter code. Friends type
  the code and press "Join room" to play together.
- Accounts: players make an account in the pop-up (or play as a guest).
- The free site handles a good number of players; if it ever gets busy we can
  move it to a bigger plan.

Your PC version (start.bat) still works exactly like before and saves accounts
in files next to server.js.
