# Bubble Break

**Bubble Break** is a free-to-play 3D arena shooter that runs right in your web browser, wrapped in a bright, bubbly Frutiger Aero / Wii U look.

**Play now:** https://bubble-break.onrender.com
(The free server naps when nobody is playing — the first visit can take about a minute to wake it up.)

## Key features
- **Portals everywhere** — place two portals and shoot, jump and flank through them
- **Fluid movement** — sprinting, sliding, wall-running, double jumps and momentum-based air control
- **Lots of modes** — Team Deathmatch, Elimination, Domination, Capture the Flag, Shotguns vs Snipers, Gun Game, Frenzy, Bomb Tag, 3v3 Lab, Free-for-all, Survival, and a rotating Arena playlist
- **5v5 online matches** — quick-play queue for public matches, private rooms with join codes, and bots that fill empty spots
- **Smart bots** — they take cover, play the objective, and learn from every match with a shared memory
- **Legends** — 9 playable characters in 5 classes (Assault, Skirmisher, Recon, Controller, Support), each with a main ability and a matching passive
- **Loadouts** — primary, secondary, melee, utility and attack items, plus weapon skins
- **Free 150-tier Battle Pass** — weapon skins, legend skins, emotes, calling cards, titles and badges in six rarities, from Common up to animated Mythics and remodelled Legendary guns
- **Progression** — levels, 39 achievements with their own rewards, daily challenges and career stats
- **Bright original world** — blue skies, glassy cities, floating islands, fish in bubbles, and indoor lab arenas
- **Original soundtrack & announcer** — reactive music, kill-streak callouts and a match-found queue sound
- **Accounts** — sign in to keep your progress on any device (every name is unique), or play as a guest

## Running it yourself
- **On your PC:** install [Node.js](https://nodejs.org), then double-click `start.bat` (or run `node server.js`) and open http://localhost:3000
- **As a website:** see `WEBSITE-SETUP.txt` (Render free web service + Upstash free Redis for accounts)
